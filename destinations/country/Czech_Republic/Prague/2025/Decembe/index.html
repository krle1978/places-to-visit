Czech Republic - Prague

Prague in December: Lanterns on the Vltava

Visited in December 2025 - Best season: Winter & Advent

A winter wander through Old Town squares, cathedral spires, and mist rising off the river.

What Did I Find?


I started at Old Town Square as the Christmas tree lit up, trumpets echoing from Tyn Church. The smell of trdelnik and mulled wine drifted past the Astronomical Clock. Lanterns hung over side streets leading to the Vltava, and every corner had a choir or violin busker adding to the Advent noise. I warmed my hands on a mug and watched the clock strike the hour with everyone cheering.
1/2
At sunrise I crossed Charles Bridge while statues disappeared into fog. The castle hill slowly appeared, so I climbed to St. Vitus for views of orange roofs dusted with frost. Later I ducked into a vaulted beer hall for goulash and dark lager, then ended the night on the riverfront watching reflections of the city lights on the water.
2/2

What to See & Do in Prague

Prague Castle & St. Vitus

Walk the courtyards, cathedral nave, and castle walls for sweeping river views.

Old Town Square

Advent market stalls, the Astronomical Clock show, and warm pastries on every corner.

Charles Bridge & Lesser Town

Cross at sunrise for statues in the mist, then wander Mala Strana's side streets.

Astronomical Clock & Tower

Watch the hourly show and climb the tower for rooftops glowing after sunset.

Best Time to Visit Prague

Prague's riverfront and squares change mood with every season:

Spring (March-May)

Calm mornings on Charles Bridge and blooming gardens in Petrin Park.

Summer (June-August)

Festival crowds, river cruises, and long evenings in beer gardens.

Autumn (September-November)

Golden leaves in Letna Park and quieter lines at castle courtyards.

Winter (December-February)

Advent markets, church concerts, and snow on the rooftops if you're lucky.
