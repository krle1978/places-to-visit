Austria - St Wolfgang im Salzkammergut

St Wolfgang in December: Advent by the Lake

Visited in December 2025 - Best season: Winter & Advent

A lakeside town glowing with Advent markets, wooden stalls, and the sound of church choirs drifting over the water.

What Did I Find?


The most beautiful atmosphere of a Christmas market by the water is offered by this small town on the shores of the lake of the same name, Wolfgang See. This place is located on the rolling slopes of the Alps, so the streets are narrow and steep. There are many shops in the streets that sell souvenirs, Christmas decorations, but also winter clothing. So, gloves and a knitted hat will not go unnoticed if you have not brought them with you.
All roads lead to the central town square, in the center of which is the church of St. Wolfgang. It is decorated with various life-size figures of priests. Around the church are stands with drinks.
1/2
I found my place by the lake, looking at the huge lantern that floats in the lake and lights up. I enjoyed the passing of tourist boats on the lake, which are decorated with colorful lights. It is a nice atmosphere, both the ride on those boats and the view of the place itself. That's what I thought, drinking green tea with honey, while a gray plush blanket was spread out on my lap and kept me warm.
I finished the tour by walking among the souvenir stands on the way to the parking lot where the tourist buses were parked.
You can visit this town for a few days and take a boat ride or take the cable car to Schafberg Mountain, or just hike the slopes around the town.

What to See & Do in St Wolfgang im Salzkammergut

Wolfgangsee Promenade

Lake views, boats, and Advent stalls in December.

Pilgrimage Church

Baroque interior and lake views from the steps.

Schafberg Cog Railway

Ride up for alpine panoramas when snow conditions allow.

Reid & Lantern Walk

Evening strolls with lanterns along the shoreline paths.

Best Time to Visit St Wolfgang im Salzkammergut

St Wolfgang shifts from summer ferries to winter lantern walks:

Spring (March-May)

Calmer shores, fresh air, and early hikes before crowds.

Summer (June-August)

Busy lake days with boats, swimming, and mountain rail rides.

Autumn (September-November)

Golden slopes and quieter evenings around the lake.

Winter (December-February)

Advent markets, choir music, and snow-dusted promenades.
