Austria - Hallstatt

Hallstatt in December: Snowy Lakeside Calm

Visited in December 2025 - Best season: Winter & Advent

Hallstatt in December is all alpine stillness: pastel houses mirrored in the lake, church bells echoing across snowy slopes, and lights from small cafes reflecting on the water.

What Did I Find?


I arrived early in the morning in Hallstatt, a small village with 800 inhabitants, which is flooded with 2,000 tourists a day. Most of them are tourists from Asia. I had the opportunity to see what the village really looks like, without people on the streets.
The town is on the shore of the lake of the same name, with a promenade that is surrounded by a wooden fence. There are three viewpoints, which I discovered, that give nice views of the city center from the lake, of the city from a height.
The village itself is a beautifully landscaped mountain village on the steep slopes of the Alps with narrow streets and a staircase that stretches uphill in a serpentine manner, connecting the private houses and cemetery above the village with the city center at the foot.
1/3

I walked along the promenade, and then I climbed up to the cemetery. From there, you can see the center of the village and the lake beautifully, as if in the palm of your hand. Since the cemetery is narrow, because it is built on a hill, the graves are short. The way people are buried here is in a kneeling position, and after 30 years, the bodies are exhumed so that other deceased people can be buried in the same grave. The bones of the exhumed ones are bleached and placed in a tomb that was built in the cemetery just for that purpose. And the names of the deceased are written on the skulls and placed in the same altar.
2/3
I finished the tour at a restaurant on the lakeside promenade. Although it was 6 degrees, I sat in the restaurant's garden and enjoyed the view of the lake. I ordered freshly caught trout from the lake.
The town began to fill with tourists. At one point, the promenade was so crowded with tourists that I couldn't even walk past them. That afternoon, my tour of Hallstatt ended.
3/3

What to See & Do in Hallstatt

Lake Promenade

Walk the waterfront for perfect reflections and calm winter light.

Skywalk Viewpoint

Ride or hike up for sweeping alpine views over the village and lake.

Old Town Alleys

Narrow streets with timber balconies, small bakeries, and local craft shops.

Church & Cemetery

Historic church with a peaceful hillside cemetery overlooking the lake.

Best Time to Visit Hallstatt

Hallstatt shifts gently with each season; here is how it feels to me:

Spring (March-May)

Snow fades from the peaks, waterfalls return, and crowds are still light.

Summer (June-August)

Busy but vibrant; boat rides, long daylight, and alpine hikes start from the village.

Autumn (September-November)

Fiery forests meet calm water; cooler air and quieter evenings.

Winter (December-February)

Silent mornings, Advent lights, and snow-muted trails above the lake.
