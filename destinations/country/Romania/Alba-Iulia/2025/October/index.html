Alba Iulia: The Heart of Transylvanian History

Visited in October 2025 • Best season: Autumn

Alba Iulia is one of the most symbolic cities in Romania — a monumental citadel, Roman ruins, elegant baroque gates and wide fortress walls create a timeless walking experience.

What Did I Find?



I arrived at a hotel near the Citadel in the evening. I shared a room with a guy I didn't know at the time, who turned out to be very funny. We went on an evening walk to the citadel together, passing the Orthodox Church, the Cathedral, and finally reaching the citadel gate.

Outside the gate, right by the bridge, there’s a pub built into the very wall of the citadel. We sat there, tried local specialties, drank local beer, and the whole evening felt light, fun, and perfectly relaxed.
1/2

The next morning, after breakfast, we joined a guided tour. While the guide explained the history of the Citadel, someone remarked that the same architect built both the Citadel and Belgrade’s Kalemegdan — to which my roommate jokingly replied, “Who did that to you?”. From that moment on, that became our motto for everything that happened around us. We explored the Citadel once again, taking photos with the life-size statues placed throughout the complex, representing figures from the era when it was built.
2/2

Why Alba Iulia is Special

▼

The Alba Carolina Citadel

A star-shaped fortress with monumental Baroque gates, rebuilt with stunning precision.

Layers of History

Roman ruins, medieval walls, baroque structures — all in one huge complex.

Wide Walking Areas

One of the cleanest, most pleasant pedestrian fortresses in Europe.

What to See & Do in Alba Iulia

Coronation Orthodox Cathedral

The place where Romania’s kings were crowned — stunning inside and out.

Roman Catholic Cathedral

One of the oldest cathedrals in Transylvania, peaceful and timeless.

Union Hall

The symbol of the 1918 union — a must-see for anyone interested in Romanian history.

The Gates of the Fortress

Seven monumental gates, each with unique sculptures and military style.

Best Time to Visit Alba Iulia

You can visit Alba Iulia all year round — the citadel is equally impressive in every season.

Spring

Perfect weather for long fortress walks.

Summer

Warm, energetic, full of outdoor events.

Autumn

Golden light, amazing for photography.

Winter

Quiet, atmospheric and very scenic.
